\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Preprocessing-equivariance contract}
Let source image $I_i$ have camera matrix
$P_i=K_i[R_i\mid t_i]$.  A controlled invertible image-space affine
$A_i\in\R^{3\times3}$ produces $I'_i$ and changes the camera to
\begin{equation}
  P'_i=A_iK_i[R_i\mid t_i].
\end{equation}
If the model applies an internal pixel map $B_i$, the complete source-to-model
map is $C_i=B_iA_i$.  We store $A_i$, $B_i$, and $C_i$ rather than infer them
after prediction.

\subsection{Common-domain comparison}
Predicted pixel-aligned quantities are mapped through $C_i^{-1}$ and compared
only on common visible support.  Relative rotations and translation directions
are evaluated over all view pairs, eliminating arbitrary world frames and
translation scale.  Depth is compared after one declared scene-level median
scale alignment.  An identity-repeat run measures the numerical noise floor.
