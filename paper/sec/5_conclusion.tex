\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Image preprocessing is part of the camera model, not merely input plumbing.
\method makes this dependency explicit and measures whether a feed-forward 3D
system respects the corresponding equivariance contract.  The current
multi-scene diagnostic establishes a reproducible VGGT failure under
view-dependent asymmetric crops while showing that symmetric transforms are
more scene dependent.  Claims about reconstruction accuracy, model generality,
failure prediction, and repair will be made only if the registered
ground-truth and second-model experiments support them.
