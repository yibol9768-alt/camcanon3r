\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Image preprocessing is part of the camera model, not merely input plumbing.
\method makes this dependency explicit and measures whether a feed-forward 3D
system respects the corresponding equivariance contract.  On 13 ETH3D scenes,
paired ground-truth evaluation establishes that view-dependent 75\% crops
degrade VGGT camera estimates, depth, and aligned point geometry, while
letterbox depth and point metrics remain statistically indistinguishable from
identity.  Matched three-scene VGGT and DUSt3R diagnostics reproduce the
cross-run signal but do not substitute for multi-model GT evidence.  The
present conclusion is therefore deliberately narrow: one registered
preprocessing family causes a real VGGT accuracy failure on ETH3D; broader
generality, failure prediction, and repair remain separate promotion gates.
