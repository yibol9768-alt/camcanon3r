\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Image preprocessing is part of the camera model, not merely input plumbing.
\method makes this dependency explicit and measures whether a feed-forward 3D
system respects the corresponding equivariance contract.  On 13 ETH3D scenes
per model, paired ground-truth evaluation establishes that off-center crops
degrade VGGT and DUSt3R cameras, depth, and point geometry.  The effect grows
with severity, persists when one crop window is shared across views, and is
amplified when windows vary independently; center and letterbox controls stay
below the registered threshold.  Model-specific and undefined metrics remain
explicit.  The present conclusion is therefore deliberately narrow: two
registered off-center crop families cause a real multi-model accuracy failure
on ETH3D.  Inverse-warping a known crop to its canonical camera canvas recovers
96.6\% and 55.8\% of the rotation-error gap for VGGT and DUSt3R with zero
measured clean cost, but it worsens depth for both models and cross-fill
consensus fails to improve the DUSt3R analytic baseline.  The supported repair
claim is therefore camera orientation only; cross-dataset generality and
held-out failure prediction remain separate promotion gates.
