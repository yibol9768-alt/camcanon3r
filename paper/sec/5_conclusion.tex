\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Image preprocessing is part of the camera model, not merely input plumbing.
\method makes this dependency explicit and measures whether a feed-forward 3D
system respects the corresponding equivariance contract. Matched multi-scene
diagnostics establish a reproducible VGGT and DUSt3R failure under
view-dependent asymmetric crops while showing that symmetric transforms are
more scene dependent. Claims about reconstruction accuracy, failure prediction,
and repair will be made only if the registered ground-truth experiments support
them.
