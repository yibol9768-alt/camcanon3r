\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Image preprocessing is part of the camera model, not merely input plumbing.
\method makes this dependency explicit and measures whether a feed-forward 3D
system respects the corresponding equivariance contract.  On 13 ETH3D scenes
per model, paired ground-truth evaluation establishes that view-dependent 75\%
crops degrade VGGT and DUSt3R camera estimates, depth, and point completeness,
while letterbox depth and point metrics remain statistically indistinguishable
from identity.  Model-specific exceptions, including DUSt3R point accuracy,
remain explicit.  The present conclusion is therefore deliberately narrow:
one registered preprocessing family causes a real multi-model accuracy failure
on ETH3D; a second transform family, cross-dataset generality, failure
prediction, and repair remain separate promotion gates.
