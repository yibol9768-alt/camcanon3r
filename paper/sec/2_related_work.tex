\paragraph{Feed-forward multi-view reconstruction.}
Classical SfM and MVS expose camera models explicitly but rely on matching,
global optimization, and/or cost-volume inference~\cite{schonberger2016sfm,
yao2018mvsnet}.  DUSt3R~\cite{wang2024dust3r},
MASt3R~\cite{leroy2024mast3r}, VGGT~\cite{wang2025vggt},
CUT3R~\cite{wang2025cut3r}, and Fast3R~\cite{yang2025fast3r} replace substantial
parts of those pipelines with learned prediction of cameras, depth, point maps,
or dense correspondence.  Their capability motivates a complementary systems
question: whether recovered 3D remains consistent when a known image-space
camera transform is introduced and removed.  We audit this contract rather
than propose another reconstruction backbone.

\paragraph{Permutation versus camera-coordinate equivariance.}
\mbox{$\pi^3$}~\cite{wang2026pi3} removes the fixed reference view used by many
feed-forward systems and builds permutation equivariance into its pose and
local-point-map representation.  It changes input order or reference-frame
choice while the image set stays fixed.  We study the complementary axis: view
identity and order stay fixed, but each image receives a known, potentially
view-dependent coordinate map.  The required transformation is $K'=AK$, not a
permutation of output slots.  Like $\pi^3$, we evaluate cameras, point maps,
depth, robustness, ablations, compute, and failure cases; unlike it, our
contribution is an audit and repair layer rather than a trained backbone.

\paragraph{Ray cameras and failure uncertainty.}
Cameras as Rays~\cite{zhang2024cameras} replaces global pose regression with a
spatial ray bundle and uses diffusion to sample plausible modes under
sparse-view ambiguity, following learned probabilistic pose estimators such as
PoseDiffusion~\cite{wang2023posediffusion}.  CamCanon3R does not learn a pose distribution:
cross-transform disagreement is a deterministic consistency score for a known
camera intervention.  We therefore compare it with native confidence on
held-out GT, report risk--coverage and oracle excess risk, and do not infer
probabilistic calibration from AUROC.  Cameras as Rays also uses tight
per-view object-box crops with corresponding ray-grid adjustment, illustrating
a concrete modern pipeline where crop coordinates are camera bookkeeping.

\paragraph{Joint pose--shape systems.}
PF-LRM~\cite{wang2024pflrm} jointly predicts pose and shape from sparse unposed
object views using image/object tokens and a differentiable PnP stage.  Its
cross-dataset, variable-view, mask-noise, model-size, and objective ablations
set a strong systems-evidence pattern.  Our task is different: PF-LRM lists
known intrinsics as an assumption, while we test the upstream boundary where
preprocessing deterministically changes those intrinsics.  We match its
systems rigor with two datasets, two distinct backbones, causal coordinate
controls, repair/compute ablations, and explicit negative outcomes rather than
manufacturing a direct leaderboard comparison between incompatible tasks.
