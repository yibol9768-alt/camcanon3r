\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Feed-forward multi-view reconstruction.}
DUSt3R~\cite{wang2024dust3r}, MASt3R~\cite{leroy2024mast3r}, and
VGGT~\cite{wang2025vggt} replace substantial parts of classical geometric
pipelines with feed-forward prediction of cameras, depth, point maps, or dense
correspondence.  Their capability motivates a complementary systems question:
whether the recovered 3D remains consistent when a known image-space camera
transform is introduced and then exactly removed.  We study this contract
rather than propose another reconstruction backbone.
