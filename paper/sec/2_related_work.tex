\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Feed-forward multi-view reconstruction.}
DUSt3R~\cite{wang2024dust3r}, MASt3R~\cite{leroy2024mast3r}, and
VGGT~\cite{wang2025vggt} replace substantial parts of classical geometric
pipelines with feed-forward prediction of cameras, depth, point maps, or dense
correspondence.  Their capability motivates a complementary systems question:
whether the recovered 3D remains consistent when a known image-space camera
transform is introduced and then exactly removed.  We study this contract
rather than propose another reconstruction backbone.

\paragraph{Equivariance and geometric consistency.}
\mbox{$\pi^3$}~\cite{wang2026pi3} removes the fixed reference view used by many
feed-forward systems and evaluates permutation equivariance by changing input
order and reference-frame selection.  This addresses an orthogonal symmetry:
we keep view identity and order fixed while applying a known, potentially
view-dependent image-coordinate map.  The required output change is then
specified by $K'=AK$, rather than by a permutation of output slots.  CamCanon3R
tests whether residual cameras and geometry agree after both the known pixel
map and the global 3D gauge are removed.

\paragraph{Camera representation, robustness, and uncertainty.}
Cameras as Rays~\cite{zhang2024cameras} replaces global pose regression with a
distributed ray bundle and uses diffusion to represent sparse-view pose
ambiguity.  PF-LRM~\cite{wang2024pflrm} jointly predicts pose and shape and
tests sensitivity to view count and segmentation noise, while conditioning on
known intrinsics.  In contrast, our intervention is neither an unknown
appearance corruption nor a sampled pose mode: it is a registered camera
change induced by image preprocessing.  We evaluate native confidence and
cross-transform disagreement as failure scores only against held-out
ground-truth error, and reserve the term uncertainty estimator until those
reliability tests succeed.
Notably, Cameras as Rays takes tight square crops around per-view object boxes
and adjusts the associated ray grid~\cite{zhang2024cameras}, illustrating a
concrete modern pipeline in which view-specific crops are camera bookkeeping,
not merely appearance augmentation.
